# Items and blocks

**Better Coppers** adds 5 items and blocks of its own, and the **Automatisation** add-on adds 20 more. Anything else a golem uses, such as copper tools and bundles, is a vanilla item.

## Better Coppers

| | Name | Type | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| <Icon id="charging_pad" /> | [Charging Pad](/items/charging_pad) | Block | Recharges a golem's energy on redstone pulses |
| <Icon id="copper_chest_minecart" /> | [Copper Chest Minecart](/items/copper_chest_minecart) | Item | A cart a golem tows on a chain to carry more |
| <Icon id="golem_handbook" /> | [Golem Handbook](/items/golem_handbook) | Item | The book that explains the mod |
| <Icon id="mineshaft_plan" /> | [Mineshaft Plan](/items/mineshaft_plan) | Item | Holds a mine's entrance, direction, length and progress |
| <Icon id="survey_table" /> | [Survey Table](/items/survey_table) | Block | Makes Mineshaft Plans |

## Automatisation

### Machines and other blocks

| | Name | Type | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| <Icon id="belt_drive" /> | [Belt Drive](/items/belt_drive) | Block | Runs a line of conveyor belts while its operator cranks |
| <Icon id="conveyor_belt" /> | [Conveyor Belt](/items/conveyor_belt) | Block | Carries items along a line |
| <Icon id="funnel" /> | [Funnel](/items/funnel) | Block | Pulls items into a container or pushes them out |
| <Icon id="chute" /> | [Chute](/items/chute) | Block | Drops items straight down |
| <Icon id="press" /> | [Press](/items/press) | Block | Turns ingots into sheets and sugar cane into paper |
| <Icon id="saw" /> | [Saw](/items/saw) | Block | Cuts sheets into gears, logs into planks, and planks and stone into shapes |
| <Icon id="grabber_arm" /> | [Grabber Arm](/items/grabber_arm) | Block | Swings items from its inputs to its outputs |
| <Icon id="golem_assembler" /> | [Golem Assembler](/items/golem_assembler) | Block | Builds new copper golems |
| <Icon id="charging_station" /> | [Charging Station](/items/charging_station) | Block | Recharges a golem with no redstone clock, faster than a Charging Pad |
