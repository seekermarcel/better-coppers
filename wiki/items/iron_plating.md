# Iron Plating

**Iron Plating** is an [upgrade](/automatisation/upgrades) that takes 75% of every hit a copper golem receives, until it has taken 40 damage.

## Obtaining

<Recipe id="iron_plating" />

## Usage

Sneak + right-click a copper golem with it. The plating can be fitted while the golem has less than 40 plating left, and each item adds 40. The maximum is therefore 79.

## Behaviour

Each hit loses 75% of its damage to the plating, up to what is left, and the rest reaches the golem. The plating value drops by that share rounded up. Damage types that bypass armour are not reduced. The plating is lost when the golem dies or turns into a statue. See [Golem upgrades](/automatisation/upgrades).
