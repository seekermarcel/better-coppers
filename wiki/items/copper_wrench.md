# Copper Wrench

A **Copper Wrench** is the job item of the [operator](/jobs/operator) and the tool for setting up [Funnels](/items/funnel) and [Grabber Arms](/items/grabber_arm). It has no durability.

## Obtaining

<Recipe id="copper_wrench" />

## Usage

| Target | Action | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Copper golem | Sneak + right-click | Hands over the wrench and makes the golem an [operator](/jobs/operator) |
| [Funnel](/items/funnel) | Right-click | Switches between pulling and pushing |
| [Grabber Arm](/items/grabber_arm) | Right-click | Starts or finishes marking its spots |
| Any block, while marking | Right-click | Sets it as input, then output, then removes it |
| Any [machine](/automatisation/machines) | Sneak + right-click | Shows its wear in percent and its repair item |

To get the wrench back, end the operator's job by sneak + right-clicking the golem with an empty hand twice within 5 seconds.
