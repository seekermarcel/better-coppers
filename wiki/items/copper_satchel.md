# Copper Satchel

A **Copper Satchel** is an [upgrade](/automatisation/upgrades) that doubles how much a [sorter](/jobs/sorter) or [courier](/jobs/courier) carries per trip, from 16 items to 32, permanently.

## Obtaining

<Recipe id="copper_satchel" />

## Usage

Sneak + right-click a copper golem with it. A golem can wear one satchel. It does nothing for other jobs.

## Behaviour

The satchel is never used up. It is the only upgrade that comes back: it drops as an item when the golem dies or turns into a statue. It is kept when the golem's job ends. See [Golem upgrades](/automatisation/upgrades).
