# Copper Coating

**Copper Coating** is an [upgrade](/automatisation/upgrades) that stops work from rusting a copper golem for 3000 energy, about three days of steady work.

## Obtaining

<Recipe id="copper_coating" />

## Usage

Sneak + right-click a copper golem with it. The coating can be fitted while the golem has less than 3000 coating left, and each item adds 3000. The maximum is therefore 5999.

## Behaviour

While coating is left, [rust](/golem/running-costs) progress does not advance from spending energy. Each point of energy spent uses up 1 coating. A golem that spends no energy keeps its coating indefinitely. The coating is lost when the golem dies or turns into a statue. See [Golem upgrades](/automatisation/upgrades).
