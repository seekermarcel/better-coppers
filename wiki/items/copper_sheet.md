# Copper Sheet

A **Copper Sheet** is a material made on the [Press](/items/press), used in recipes, cut into gears and used as the [Belt Drive](/items/belt_drive)'s repair part.

## Obtaining

| Machine | Input | Output |
|---|---|---|
| [Press](/items/press) | 1 Copper Ingot | 1 Copper Sheet |

## Usage

<Recipe uses="copper_sheet" />

The [Saw](/items/saw) cuts 1 Copper Sheet into 1 [Copper Gear](/items/copper_gear). 4 Copper Sheets are needed per golem in the [Golem Assembler](/items/golem_assembler). A [Belt Drive](/items/belt_drive) is [repaired](/automatisation/wear-and-jams) with 1 Copper Sheet.

## Advancements

| Flat Out | Have a Copper, Iron or Gold Sheet in the inventory |
