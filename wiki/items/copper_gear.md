# Copper Gear

A **Copper Gear** is a material cut from a [Copper Sheet](/items/copper_sheet) on the [Saw](/items/saw).

## Obtaining

| Machine | Input | Output |
|---|---|---|
| [Saw](/items/saw) | 1 Copper Sheet | 1 Copper Gear |

## Usage

<Recipe uses="copper_gear" />

2 Copper Gears are also needed per golem in the [Golem Assembler](/items/golem_assembler).
