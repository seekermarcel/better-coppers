# Golem Handbook

The **Golem Handbook** is the in-game book that explains the mod chapter by chapter. It is written in English and German and follows the game language.

## Obtaining

Every player gets one the first time they join a world, from the mod's root [advancement](/reference/advancements). It is in the mod's creative tab. A lost copy can be crafted:

<Recipe id="golem_handbook" />

## Usage

Use the book to open it. It opens in the vanilla book screen with a contents page, and chapters from add-ons follow the base mod's chapters.

## Chapters

| # | Chapter | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Welcome | Your first golem, the three steps to a job, the guide advancements |
| 2 | Homes and Status | Linking, status, ending and changing jobs |
| 3 | Sorter | Where items go, item frames, working stock |
| 4 | Copper Chest Minecart | Chaining and related items |
| 5 | Miner | Mineshaft Plans, supplies |
| 6 | Farmer | Fields and seeds |
| 7 | Forester, Fisher, Keeper, Crafter | Those four jobs |
| 8 | Courier | Routes |
| 9 | Minecarts and Boats | Rides |
| 10 | Monsters and Iron Golems | Hunting, guards, repairs |
| 11 | Energy and Rust | Running costs |
| 12 | Tools and Eyes | Copper tools, broken tools, full chests, eyes |
| 13 | Armour Trims | Giving and removing trims |
| 14 | Quick Reference | Job items, clicks, energy, eyes |
