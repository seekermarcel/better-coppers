# Iron Sheet

An **Iron Sheet** is a material made on the [Press](/items/press), used in recipes and as the repair part of most [machines](/automatisation/machines).

## Obtaining

| Machine | Input | Output |
|---|---|---|
| [Press](/items/press) | 1 Iron Ingot | 1 Iron Sheet |

## Usage

<Recipe uses="iron_sheet" />

The [Press](/items/press), [Saw](/items/saw), [Grabber Arm](/items/grabber_arm) and [Golem Assembler](/items/golem_assembler) are [repaired](/automatisation/wear-and-jams) with 1 Iron Sheet. Iron Sheets are not cut into gears.

| Flat Out | Have a Copper, Iron or Gold Sheet in the inventory |
