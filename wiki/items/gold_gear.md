A **Gold Gear** is a material cut from a [Gold Sheet](/items/gold_sheet) on the [Saw](/items/saw), used for [Gold Gearwork](/items/gold_gearwork).

## Obtaining

| Machine | Input | Output |
|---|---|---|
| [Saw](/items/saw) | 1 Gold Sheet | 1 Gold Gear |
