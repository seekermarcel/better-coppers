# Gold Sheet

A **Gold Sheet** is a material made on the [Press](/items/press), used for the [Charging Station](/items/charging_station) and cut into [Gold Gears](/items/gold_gear).

## Obtaining

| Machine | Input | Output |
|---|---|---|
| [Press](/items/press) | 1 Gold Ingot | 1 Gold Sheet |

## Usage

<Recipe uses="gold_sheet" />

The [Saw](/items/saw) cuts 1 Gold Sheet into 1 [Gold Gear](/items/gold_gear).

## Advancements

| Advancement | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Flat Out | Have a Copper, Iron or Gold Sheet in the inventory |
