# Your first golem

**Your first golem** takes four steps: build a [copper golem](/golem/copper-golem), give it a [job item](/jobs/), [link](/start/homes-and-status) it to a copper chest, and give it something to do. This walkthrough makes a [sorter](/jobs/sorter), the simplest job, because it has no [running costs](/golem/running-costs).

The [Golem Handbook](/items/golem_handbook) covers the same steps, and the Better Coppers tab in the advancements screen (L) leads from one step to the next.

## 1. Build the golem

Place a carved pumpkin on top of a copper block. The block can be any weathering stage, waxed or not. This is the vanilla recipe, described on the [Minecraft Wiki](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Copper_Golem): the golem appears, and the copper block turns into a copper chest of the same stage. That chest can be the golem's home.

A golem that was just built is an **untouched golem**. It behaves exactly as in vanilla until a player acts on it: see [the vanilla rule](/start/vanilla-rule).

## 2. Give it a job item

Hold the job item and **sneak + right-click** the golem. For a sorter, the job item is a bundle (of any colour). The golem keeps it in its off hand and the bundle is used up from the player's hand.

| Job | Job item | Also needs |
|---|---|---|
| [Sorter](/jobs/sorter) | <Icon id="bundle" /> Bundle | A home |
| [Miner](/jobs/miner) | <Icon id="copper_pickaxe" /> Copper pickaxe, then a marked [Mineshaft Plan](/items/mineshaft_plan) | A home, supplies |
| [Farmer](/jobs/farmer) | <Icon id="copper_hoe" /> Copper hoe | A home, a field |
| [Forester](/jobs/forester) | <Icon id="copper_axe" /> Copper axe | A home, trees |
| [Fisher](/jobs/fisher) | <Icon id="fishing_rod" /> Fishing rod | A home, open water |
| [Animal keeper](/jobs/animal-keeper) | <Icon id="brush" /> Brush | A home, animals, food |
| [Beekeeper](/jobs/beekeeper) | <Icon id="shears" /> Shears | A home, full hives over a lit campfire |
| [Crafter](/jobs/crafter) | <Icon id="crafting_table" /> Crafting table | A home, item frames, ingredients |
| [Courier](/jobs/courier) | None: a route between two containers | A route instead of a home |
| [Operator](/jobs/operator) (add-on) | <Icon id="copper_wrench" /> [Copper Wrench](/items/copper_wrench) | A machine |

Pickaxes, axes and hoes must be copper: see [Tools](/golem/tools). A plain right-click never hands anything over. All clicks are listed under [Controls](/start/controls).

If the golem has no home yet, a message above the hotbar says what to do next.

## 3. Link it to a copper chest

1. Sneak + right-click the golem with an **empty hand**. It glows, and the chat shows its job and home. The pick lasts 30 seconds.
2. Right-click the copper chest. The chest must be within 32 blocks of the golem.

A dotted line of copper particles runs from the golem to the chest, and a message above the hotbar confirms the home. The steps can be done in the other order: a golem can be linked first and given its job afterwards. Details and limits are under [Homes and status](/start/homes-and-status).

## 4. Give it something to do

Put items in the copper chest. Place chests or barrels within 32 blocks of the golem, and within 8 blocks up or down. The sorter empties its home chest and carries each item to where it belongs. The rules for where each item goes are on the [sorter](/jobs/sorter) page.

## Checking on it

| Action | Result |
|---|---|
| Right-click a golem with a job, empty hand | Shows its status above the hotbar and changes nothing |
| Hold sneak | Copper particles run from each golem within 24 blocks to its home |
| Sneak + right-click twice with an empty hand, within 5 seconds | Ends the job and returns the job item |

The [status message](/start/homes-and-status#status-message) is explained part by part.

## Paying jobs

Every job except sorter and courier is a **paying job**: it uses energy, makes the golem rust and wears out its tool. A golem with a paying job needs a [Charging Pad](/items/charging_pad) near its home, driven by a redstone clock, and spare copper tools in a chest nearby. See [Running costs](/golem/running-costs). The golem stops working at night and goes to its pad.

Monsters also hunt golems that have a job, of any kind. An iron golem nearby keeps them safe: see [Monsters](/golem/monsters) and [Guards and repairs](/golem/guards-and-repairs).
