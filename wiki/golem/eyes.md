# Eyes

**Eyes** are what a [copper golem](/golem/copper-golem) gets from a block of iron, gold or diamond, widening its work radius. Eyes are not an [add-on upgrade](/automatisation/upgrades); they are part of the base mod. They glow in the colour of the block used.

<!-- MEDIA: eyes-tiers.png | screenshot | Four copper golems side by side: plain eyes, iron eyes, gold eyes and diamond eyes -->

## Giving eyes

Sneak + right-click a golem with the block. The block is used up. A golem of any kind can take eyes, except a [courier](/jobs/courier), which refuses with a message: couriers have no radius.

| Block | Multiplier | Message shows |
|---|---|---|
| [Block of Iron](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Block_of_Iron) | 1.5 times | 150% |
| [Block of Gold](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Block_of_Gold) | 2 times | 200% |
| [Block of Diamond](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Block_of_Diamond) | 3 times | 300% |

The golem's status message shows the eyes as a percentage.

## What eyes widen

| Radius | Base | Iron | Gold | Diamond |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Farmer](/jobs/farmer) field | 8 | 12 | 16 | 24 |
| [Forester](/jobs/forester) forest | 12 | 18 | 24 | 36 |
| [Fisher](/jobs/fisher) water | 10 | 15 | 20 | 30 |
| [Animal keeper](/jobs/animal-keeper) animals | 10 | 15 | 20 | 30 |
| [Sorter](/jobs/sorter) containers | 32 | 48 | 64 | 96 |
| [Miner](/jobs/miner) vein budget (ore blocks per vein) | 16 | 24 | 32 | 48 |
| [Operator](/jobs/operator) spare parts (add-on) | 8 | 12 | 16 | 24 |

Vertical reach is not widened: it stays at 3 blocks for a farmer, 6 for a forester and 8 for a sorter. Eyes do not change the range of charging, tool fetching or repairs.

## Replacing and losing

Giving a different block replaces the eyes, in either direction. The old block goes back to the player's inventory, or lands at the golem's feet if there is no room. Giving the block the golem already has does nothing.

Eyes stay when a job ends or changes. The block drops when the golem [dies or turns into a statue](/golem/monsters#death).
