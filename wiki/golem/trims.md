# Armour trims

A **trim** is an [armour trim](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Smithing_Template) worn by a [copper golem](/golem/copper-golem), purely for looks. It changes nothing about a golem's work, health or speed.

<!-- MEDIA: trims-golem.png | screenshot | A copper golem wearing a trim in a clearly visible colour, such as a gold sentry trim, next to one without a trim -->

## Giving a trim

1. Sneak + right-click the golem with an armour trim smithing template. The template goes onto the golem. The player sees "Template on. Now sneak + right-click the golem with a trim material (iron, gold, amethyst...)."
2. Sneak + right-click the golem with a trim material. The material is used up and the trim appears in that material's colour.

A golem of any kind can wear a trim. Without a template on, trim materials are ignored, so handing a golem an iron ingot does not start a trim.

### Patterns

All 18 vanilla patterns work: Bolt, Coast, Dune, Eye, Flow, Host, Raiser, Rib, Sentry, Shaper, Silence, Snout, Spire, Tide, Vex, Ward, Wayfinder and Wild.

### Materials

All 11 vanilla trim materials work: amethyst, copper, diamond, emerald, gold, iron, lapis, netherite, quartz, redstone and resin.

## Changing a trim

Sneak + right-click with a different template or material. A new template replaces the old one, which goes back to the player, and the trim keeps its material. A new material replaces the old one; the old material is not returned. Using the same template or material again does nothing and uses nothing.

## Taking a trim off

Sneak + right-click with [shears](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Shears). The trim is removed and the template is given back; the shears lose 1 durability. The material is lost.

If the golem wears a flower on its antenna, the flower comes off first, as in vanilla. A plain click with shears is also vanilla and does not touch the trim.

## Keeping and losing

A trim stays when a job ends. When a golem [dies or turns into a statue](/golem/monsters#death), only the template drops.
