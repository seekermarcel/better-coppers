# A plain click on a golem is safe; giving it something takes a sneak

Vanilla throws a copper golem's held item at any player who clicks it with an empty hand. For a golem with a job that item is its tool, so one careless click would end the job, and a click with a job item would replace it. So with a job, a plain empty-hand click only shows the status, and cancels a link the player started on that golem. Without one it stays exactly vanilla, and shears, honeycomb and axes stay vanilla for every golem, with one difference on a golem with a job: vanilla also throws when a plain axe or an inert item did nothing and the empty off hand gets its turn, and a guard stops that throw, while scraping still works. Job items, plans, seeds, eyes, trims, shears for taking a trim off, wrenches and upgrades all take sneak + right-click, and a second press of sneak + empty hand within 5 seconds ends a job, after the first click's message said so; without a job it cancels the link, as a plain click has to stay vanilla there. Clicks within 5 ticks count as one press, because a held button repeats them. The cost is a click players have to learn, and it differs from vanilla's habit of handing things over directly.

The exception is a player leading a chained cart, who hands it over with a plain empty-hand click: chaining the cart first already says what they mean. The client does not know a golem's job and runs vanilla's throw anyway, so the server resends the golem's equipment after blocking a click.
